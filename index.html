uBUFF Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 13, 2026

In short: uBUFF processes your game data locally on your PC. The core AI companion runs on your machine. Optional premium features may send limited data (such as game screenshots) to cloud AI services for analysis. We do not sell your data.

1. What Is uBUFF

uBUFF is a companion app for PC games, built on the Overwolf (ow-electron) platform. It reads your local game save files and provides real-time game intelligence on a second monitor. uBUFF offers a free ad-supported tier as well as optional paid subscription tiers with additional features.

2. What Data We Access

uBUFF accesses the following data, all of which stays on your local machine:

  • Game save files — uBUFF reads save files from your local filesystem (e.g., .ess for Skyrim, .sfs for Starfield, encrypted CD saves). These files are parsed locally and are never uploaded to any server.
  • User preferences — Settings and parsed game state are stored locally in %APPDATA%/ubuff/.
  • Local AI processing — The AI companion runs entirely on your machine via Ollama (llama3.2:3b). No prompts, game data, or AI responses are transmitted externally. The AI works fully offline.

3. Screen Capture & Cloud AI

uBUFF may capture screenshots of your game screen to provide enhanced AI-powered analysis. When cloud AI features are enabled (available on paid tiers), these screenshots are sent to third-party AI services for processing. Screenshots are used solely for gameplay analysis and are not stored permanently on our servers or used for any other purpose.

  • Screenshot capture is used only for AI gameplay analysis and is clearly indicated when active.
  • Cloud AI processing is available on paid subscription tiers. Free-tier users use the local AI only, with no data leaving the device.
  • We do not hook into game memory or inject code into game processes.
  • We do not sell, share, or use your game data for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Account & Authentication

An account is required to use uBUFF. Account data (email address and authentication credentials) is stored securely via our backend service. Account information is used solely for authentication, subscription management, and delivering app features.

5. Third-Party Services

Overwolf Platform: uBUFF runs within the Overwolf/ow-electron framework. Overwolf may collect platform-level data (such as app usage statistics) as described in the Overwolf Privacy Policy. uBUFF itself does not control or have access to data collected by the Overwolf platform.

Advertising: uBUFF displays ads through Overwolf's built-in ad system (owadview). Overwolf and its ad partners handle ad serving and may collect standard ad-related data (such as ad impressions and device identifiers) in accordance with their own privacy policies. uBUFF does not independently collect or transmit any user data for ad targeting.

6. Data Storage

All user data is stored locally on your computer in %APPDATA%/ubuff/. No data is stored on remote servers. You can delete this folder at any time to remove all uBUFF data from your system.

7. Children's Privacy & Age Appropriateness

uBUFF is not directed at children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. uBUFF is a companion tool for PC games, many of which carry mature content ratings (e.g., ESRB Mature 17+). uBUFF does not control or rate game content; the age-appropriateness of each supported game is determined by the game's publisher and rating board. Parents and guardians should review the ratings of games their children play. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. If we introduce features that collect data in future versions, we will update this policy before those features are released.
