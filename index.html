This Privacy Policy applies to:

3. Who Can Use uBUFF

uBUFF is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us personal information, contact us at ubuff.official@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

4. Data Practices

4.1 Account Data

When you create an account, we collect and store via Supabase:

  • Email address
  • Hashed password (we never see your plaintext password)
  • Account creation and last-login timestamps
  • Subscription status, if and when paid tiers are offered

4.2 Local Data Accessed by uBUFF

Under Section 3.1 of our Terms of Service, you authorize uBUFF to read files on your computer that are relevant to operating the service, including game save files, configuration files, log files, and plug-in metadata. As of the Effective Date, the content of these files is processed exclusively on your computer by local software and is not transmitted to us or to any third-party AI service. We reserve the right to introduce cloud-based processing in future versions; any such change will be accompanied by an update to this Policy and, where required by law, your affirmative consent.

4.3 Usage Data

When you use the app, we collect non-content usage signals:

  • Advice request events (timestamp, game identifier — e.g., "skyrim-se")
  • Feature interaction events (e.g., voice input used, map opened)
  • Session start/end timestamps
  • App version, operating system, and hardware class (for debugging and compatibility)

These signals help us maintain the service and detect abuse. They do not contain the content of your game data or your conversations.

4.4 Advertising Data

uBUFF is ad-supported. Third-party advertising networks (including the Overwolf advertising platform) may collect, through their own technology operating inside the uBUFF application, data necessary to select, display, and measure advertisements, which may include advertising identifiers, approximate geographic location, session duration, and ad impression and click events. This data is collected and processed by the ad network under its own privacy policy. uBUFF receives only aggregate reporting and does not receive user-level advertising data.

4.5 Payment Data (if applicable)

If uBUFF introduces paid services in a future version, payment processing will be handled by a third-party payment processor (currently Tebex). We will not collect or store credit card numbers or payment card information. We will receive only subscription status confirmations from the processor.

4.6 Technical and Diagnostic Data

  • IP address (collected by hosting infrastructure for authentication, rate-limiting, and abuse prevention; retained per our hosting providers' policies)
  • App error logs and crash reports. Crash reports may include technical context such as stack traces, operating-system version, and uBUFF version. We take reasonable steps to avoid collecting your personal file contents in these reports, but we cannot guarantee their complete exclusion.

4.7 Data We Do Not Collect

Except as described above, uBUFF does not:

  • Read memory from any running game process
  • Record keystrokes or mouse input
  • Capture, record, or transmit your in-game voice or text communications
  • Sell your personal data to data brokers

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access — Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion — Request deletion of your account and associated data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Data portability — Request your data in a portable format (where technically feasible)
  • Objection — Object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Restriction — Request that we restrict processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at ubuff.official@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Note for EU/EEA users (GDPR): You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority if you believe we have violated your rights.

Note for California users (CCPA): You have the right to know what personal information we collect, delete it, and opt out of its sale. We do not sell personal information.

9. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data we control:

  • Passwords are hashed using industry-standard methods via Supabase Auth
  • Data in transit between uBUFF and our servers is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS
  • Access to production systems is restricted to authorized personnel
  • In the current version, the content of your save files is processed locally on your computer and is not transmitted to us

No system is perfectly secure, and we make no representation or warranty that our security measures will prevent all unauthorized access. If we become aware of a data breach that affects your rights, we will notify you in accordance with applicable law.

10. Cookies and Local Storage

The uBUFF application may use local storage on your computer to retain:

  • Your logged-in session token
  • App preferences (selected game, UI settings, onboarding state)
  • Caches used by the local AI runtime and uBUFF's own data files

uBUFF does not itself use tracking cookies for advertising. However, third-party advertising networks embedded in uBUFF (see Section 4.4) may use their own identifiers, cookies, or device-storage mechanisms for ad selection and measurement, governed by their own privacy policies.

11. International Data Transfers

uBUFF is operated from the United States. If you are located outside the US, your data may be transferred to and processed in the United States. By using uBUFF, you consent to this transfer. Where required by law (e.g., GDPR), we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or other lawful transfer mechanisms.

12. Children

uBUFF is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you are under 18, do not use this service.
